# Immunitywize — logo concepts & generation prompts

Eight logo directions for **Immunitywize**, each with the story behind it, how the mark is built, how it holds up from a 16 px favicon to a shop banner, and ready-to-use prompts for any image model (Midjourney, GPT-Image, Ideogram, Imagen, Flux, Recraft).

These are **concept prompts, not final artwork**. Pick 2–3 directions, generate 20–40 variations of each, choose the strongest, and have a designer redraw the winner as clean vector. Image models can't produce production-ready logos, but they're very good at helping you find one.

---

## What the logo has to carry

| Truth about the brand | What it means for the logo |
|---|---|
| Food made **by hand, at home**, by family | It should feel touched by a person, not generated by a machine. One confident line beats ten perfect shapes. |
| **Slow** (roasting until it smells right) | Calm, unhurried forms. Nothing sharp, aggressive or "tech". |
| Recipes passed **between generations**, especially mothers | Warmth and lineage, so it feels inherited rather than invented. |
| **Protection and care** ("immunity" in the everyday, grandmotherly sense, not the clinical one) | Gestures of guarding and nourishing, never medical crosses, shields-with-ticks, capsules or DNA. |
| Indian, but **premium and modern** | Draw on household objects and rituals everyone recognises, made with the restraint of a modern studio. Avoid clip-art paisleys, generic mandalas, Om symbols and "Indian font" clichés. |

### Must-pass tests for every concept

1. **16 px test:** at favicon size it still reads as a distinct shape, not a smudge.
2. **One-colour test:** works in a single colour, as a debossed lid, a rubber stamp, embroidery, and a laser-etched steel tin.
3. **Seal test:** fits inside a circle, for jar-lid stickers, social avatars and wax/lac seals.
4. **App-icon test:** fits a rounded square with the mark at ~60% size and doesn't need the name.
5. **No-name test:** if you cover the word Immunitywize, the symbol should still say something about home, hands, slowness or care.
6. **Motion test:** it can animate in under 2 seconds in a way that has meaning (drawing on, stirring, tying). The website already uses a line-drawing animation, so a mark that can be "drawn" fits the brand.

### Wordmark guidance (shared by all concepts)

- Set **Immunitywize** as one word in a warm, high-contrast serif with soft, slightly calligraphic terminals. Current site fonts: Cormorant Garamond for display, Hanken Grotesk for UI. Good references are Cormorant, Canela, GT Super, Ogg and Reckless.
- Option: set **"wize"** in the italic of the same family. It gives a gentle, knowing lift, a wink at the playful "z", without changing font.
- Tracking: slightly open (+10 to +20) in all caps for packaging bands; normal tracking in sentence case for the website header.
- Image models garble text. **Generate the symbol on its own, then set the wordmark with real type.** Ideogram and GPT-Image are the most reliable if you want to preview lockups.

### Palette to test against (from the website)

Espresso `#2E2119` · Ivory `#FBF8F3` · Saffron `#9C4A22` · Turmeric `#D9A441` · Cardamom `#3F5A2E` · Sand `#F3EDE4`.
Try every concept in **espresso on ivory**, **ivory on espresso**, and **saffron on sand**.

### Universal negative prompt (append to every prompt)

```
no gradients, no 3D, no bevel, no glossy effects, no drop shadows, no photorealism in the logo itself, no mockup clutter, no extra text, no misspelled text, no random letters, no watermark, no stock-icon style, no clip art, no generic mandala, no Om symbol, no paisley, no medical cross, no shield with checkmark, no capsule, no DNA helix, no leaf-in-circle cliché, no cartoon mascot, no emoji style, no hairline strokes that disappear at small sizes
```

## 1 · Angootha — "Signed by hand"

**The story.** A generation of Indian grandmothers who never learned to write signed letters, land papers and money orders with their **thumbprint** (*angootha*). It was their signature, their promise and their identity. Every Immunitywize batch is made by hand, so the thumbprint becomes the brand's signature: *this was made by a person, and she stands behind it.*

**The mark.** A circular seal holding a stylised thumbprint drawn in 7–9 smooth concentric ridges. The ridges aren't perfect ellipses: they drift like a real print. At the top, one ridge breaks free and rises out of the seal as a **single curl of steam**: the kitchen is still warm.

**Construction.**
- Outer ring: optional, used for seal/sticker versions; omitted in the website header.
- Ridges: uniform stroke at about 6% of the mark's width, rounded ends, with two deliberate breaks so it reads as a fingerprint rather than a target.
- Steam curl: the same stroke weight, an S-curve rising about 35% above the print.
- Reduced version under 32 px: 4 ridges plus the curl.

**Across touchpoints.** A circle makes the perfect jar-lid sticker, lac seal on gift boxes, app icon and Instagram avatar. Embossed on kraft paper it feels genuinely handmade, and it can be hand-stamped on every box as part of the unboxing ritual.

**Motion.** The ridges draw on from the centre outward, then the steam curl lifts and gently sways. Use it as the website loader or the order-confirmation moment: "your batch has been signed".

**Prompt — symbol**
```
A minimalist logo symbol for a premium homemade Indian snack brand: a stylised human thumbprint made of 8 smooth, slightly irregular concentric ridges drawn with a single confident uniform line weight and rounded line ends, like a hand-inked stamp; one ridge at the top breaks free and rises out of the print as a single elegant curl of steam. Centered, generous negative space, flat vector, solid espresso brown #2E2119 on warm ivory #FBF8F3. Heartfelt, human, quiet and premium, like a heritage seal redrawn by a modern design studio. Logo presentation, single mark only.
```
**Prompt — seal on packaging**
```
Product photo of a kraft paper jar label and a deep saffron-red lac wax seal pressed onto the lid of a glass jar of golden panjiri; the seal is embossed with a minimalist thumbprint made of concentric ridges with a single curl of steam rising from the top. Soft natural daylight, warm ivory and sand tones, shallow depth of field, editorial packaging photography, premium artisanal Indian food brand, no readable text.
```
**Prompt — app icon**
```
App icon, rounded square, solid espresso brown background #2E2119, centered ivory line-art mark: a stylised thumbprint of 5 smooth concentric ridges with one ridge rising as a curl of steam, uniform bold stroke, perfectly legible at small sizes, flat, no text, premium minimal.
```

## 2 · The Slow Stir — "Roasted until it smells right"

**The story.** Panjiri can't be rushed: the atta is stirred over a low flame for a long time, until the kitchen fills with a nutty smell. Patience is the real secret ingredient. This mark is the path a spoon traces in a kadhai, seen from above.

**The mark.** A kadhai seen from directly above: a circle with two small loop handles on either side. Inside, **one continuous spiral** (the stirring path) is drawn without lifting the pen. Where the spiral reaches the centre, its end turns upward into a small **sprouting leaf**: patience turning into nourishment.

**Construction.**
- The two side handles give the circle a unique silhouette, so it isn't "just a circle", and stay readable at 16 px.
- 2½ spiral turns, uniform stroke, never touching itself.
- The leaf is the only filled shape, so the eye is drawn to it.
- The horizontal lockup (mark + wordmark) is natural because the handles point outward like arms.

**Across touchpoints.** Recognisable at a glance, it fits a circle and works as a debossed lid. The spiral also becomes a **pattern**: repeated spirals on tissue paper and box interiors.

**Motion.** The spiral draws itself in a slow stirring motion (about 1.6 s), then the leaf unfurls. This directly matches the scroll-drawn kitchen animation already on the website.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist logo symbol, top-down view of an Indian kadhai (wok) drawn as a circle with two small round loop handles on the left and right; inside it a single continuous hand-drawn spiral line traces the path of a stirring spoon, 2.5 turns, never crossing itself, ending at the centre where the line lifts into a small sprouting leaf (the only filled shape). Uniform confident stroke, rounded ends, flat vector, espresso #2E2119 on ivory #FBF8F3, lots of breathing room, calm and premium, artisanal food brand, single mark only.
```
**Prompt — pattern**
```
Seamless packaging pattern made from a minimalist line icon of a kadhai seen from above with a spiral stirring path ending in a small leaf, repeated in a loose staggered grid, saffron #9C4A22 lines on sand #F3EDE4, printed inside a premium kraft gift box, top-down product photo, soft daylight, no text.
```
**Prompt — lockup preview (Ideogram / GPT-Image)**
```
Horizontal logo lockup: on the left a minimalist line icon of a kadhai seen from above with a spiral stirring line ending in a tiny leaf; on the right the word "Immunitywize" set in an elegant high-contrast serif with soft terminals, with the last four letters "wize" in italic. Espresso brown on ivory, generous spacing, flat, premium, centered on a plain background.
```

## 3 · Kalava — "Tied with care"

**The story.** In Indian homes the red-and-yellow sacred thread, *kalava* or *mauli*, is tied around the wrist by a mother, grandmother or priest as a quiet act of protection. It's the most intimate everyday symbol of **being looked after**, which is exactly what "immunity" means here: not a clinical promise, but a family's care.

**The mark.** A single thread drawn as one line forms a small, soft **knot** with two loops. Looking closely, the loops form a gentle **heart** and the trailing ends form a **"w"**. In the full logo, the thread runs beneath the wordmark like an underline and ties itself in the knot after "wize".

**Construction.**
- Two-colour version: a saffron-red line, plus a thin turmeric twin line that separates only at the knot, echoing the real two-tone thread.
- One-colour version: a single line, which must still read as a knot.
- No more than two crossings, so it stays legible at small sizes.

**Across touchpoints.** The knot alone is a distinctive, emotional app icon and social avatar. On packaging, the thread wraps around the jar as a printed band that "ties" at the front, and gift boxes can use a real red cotton thread tied the same way.

**Motion.** The thread draws in from the left under the name, loops, and pulls itself tight into the knot with a tiny bounce.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist logo mark of a single soft thread tied into a small knot with two loops, drawn as one continuous uniform line with rounded ends; the two loops gently suggest a heart and the trailing ends form a subtle lowercase "w". Inspired by the Indian sacred protective wrist thread (kalava / mauli). Two thin parallel lines in deep saffron red #9C4A22 and turmeric #D9A441 that run together and separate only at the knot. Flat vector on ivory #FBF8F3, tender, heartfelt, premium, no other elements.
```
**Prompt — packaging**
```
Glass jar of homemade dry-fruit laddus with a printed paper band wrapped around the middle; a fine red-and-turmeric line illustration of a thread circles the jar and ties into a small soft knot at the front; kraft paper, brass lid, warm natural light, editorial packaging photography, premium Indian artisanal brand, no readable text.
```
**Prompt — app icon**
```
App icon, rounded square, warm ivory background, centered minimalist knot made of a single bold saffron-red line with two soft loops forming a heart shape, flat, bold enough to read at 32 pixels, no text.
```

## 4 · Tulsi Vrindavan — "The guardian of the courtyard"

**The story.** In many Indian homes a **tulsi plant** grows in a small raised planter, the *tulsi vrindavan*, in the courtyard or on the balcony. Every evening someone lights a small lamp in its niche. It is the home's quiet guardian, cared for daily. Here it symbolises everyday care; it is not a health claim.

**The mark.** The planter's distinctive **stepped silhouette** (base, body with a small arched niche, crown) is drawn as an outline. Three **tulsi leaves** grow out of the top. In the arched niche sits a tiny **diya flame**, which is also shaped like a leaf. The overall silhouette deliberately resembles a **jar**, linking the home's guardian to the product.

**Construction.**
- A symmetrical silhouette with three steps at the base; the arch niche sits at 45% height.
- Leaves: three almond shapes, the middle one tallest.
- The flame/leaf in the niche is the only filled element, in saffron in the two-colour version.

**Across touchpoints.** A tall, iconic silhouette, unlike any snack brand. It's great on the jar label, and centred it works for Instagram highlights and the app icon.

**Motion.** The lamp flickers on in the niche, then the three leaves unfurl upward one after another.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist emblem of an Indian tulsi vrindavan (the raised courtyard planter for the holy basil plant): symmetrical stepped silhouette with a small arched niche, drawn in a single uniform outline; three almond-shaped tulsi leaves grow from the top, the centre one tallest; inside the arched niche a tiny diya flame shaped like a leaf, filled in saffron #9C4A22; the overall silhouette subtly resembles a glass jar. Flat vector, espresso #2E2119 outline on ivory, calm, modern and secular-feeling, premium food brand mark, no text.
```
**Prompt — stationery**
```
Blind-debossed logo on a thick ivory cotton-paper gift card: a minimalist stepped tulsi planter silhouette with three leaves growing from the top and a tiny flame in an arched niche; soft raking daylight revealing the deboss, premium stationery photography, no readable text.
```

## 5 · Kolam — "Drawn fresh every morning"

**The story.** Across South India, and in rangoli traditions everywhere, someone draws a **kolam** at the doorstep every morning: a grid of dots wrapped by **one unbroken line**. It's made fresh daily and washed away, a quiet welcome and blessing for the home. It perfectly mirrors small-batch food: made fresh, by hand, every day, with care for whoever crosses the threshold.

**The mark.** A **3×3 grid of dots** (the dots read as seeds, nuts and grains) wrapped by a single continuous line that loops around them without breaking. The loop is designed so its overall shape quietly forms a rounded **"w"**, or a protective cupped form.

**Construction.**
- Follow real kolam rules: the line never crosses itself at a dot, every dot is enclosed, and the line is closed.
- Dots in saffron, line in espresso (two colours), or all one colour.
- Under 24 px: drop to a 2×2 grid.

**Across touchpoints.** Mathematically elegant and very scalable. It extends into a **system**: each product line gets its own kolam (panjiri 3×3, laddus 2×3, mixes 4×4), so the range feels like one family. It's also beautiful as a pattern.

**Motion.** The line draws itself around the dots in one stroke, like a hand drawing chalk on a doorstep at dawn.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist logo mark inspired by a South Indian kolam: a 3 by 3 grid of small round dots in saffron #9C4A22, wrapped by one single continuous closed line in espresso #2E2119 that loops around every dot without crossing at the dots, smooth rounded curves, uniform stroke; the overall loop forms a soft shape that subtly suggests a lowercase "w" or cupped hands. Flat vector on ivory #FBF8F3, mathematically elegant, calm, heartfelt, premium, no text.
```
**Prompt — product-line system**
```
A family of three minimalist kolam-style icons for product packaging: dot grids of 3x3, 2x3 and 4x4 each wrapped by a single continuous line, same stroke weight, arranged side by side on sand-coloured paper, espresso lines and saffron dots, design system presentation board, flat, no text.
```

## 6 · Masala Dabba — "The heart of every kitchen"

**The story.** Every Indian kitchen has a round steel **masala dabba**: one tin, seven small bowls, six around one. Grandmothers cooked by feel from it, a pinch of this and a little of that. It's the original "customise your batch": the same ingredients, balanced differently for each family.

**The mark.** A top-down **circle holding seven smaller circles**: six around a centre. Each small circle has a different minimal texture (dots, a short stroke, a crescent, a seed shape) suggesting a different ingredient. The **centre circle** is solid saffron, a laddu or a sun. The arrangement also reads as a **flower**, or a protective cell.

**Construction.**
- Strict hexagonal geometry, so it feels crafted rather than random.
- Textures are optional: drop them below 48 px and keep just the seven circles, centre filled.
- The outer ring has a tiny lip notch at the top, a nod to the dabba's lid.

**Across touchpoints.** Very strong and simple at small sizes, and ideal for a round app icon. It connects naturally to the website's batch builder, where the seven bowls could light up as the user adds ingredients.

**Motion.** The seven bowls fill one by one, in a circle, finishing with the centre.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist logo symbol of an Indian masala dabba seen from directly above: an outer circle containing seven smaller circles in a perfect hexagonal arrangement (six around one); each outer small circle holds a different tiny minimal texture (fine dots, a single short stroke, a crescent, a seed shape, cross-hatching, three tiny lines); the centre circle is solid saffron #9C4A22 like a sweet laddu or a sun; uniform espresso outlines on ivory; reads like a flower; flat vector, precise, warm, premium, no text.
```
**Prompt — etched tin**
```
Laser-etched minimalist logo of seven circles in a hexagonal arrangement inside a ring, on the lid of a round stainless steel tin, top-down product shot on grey stone, soft daylight, premium artisanal food packaging, no readable text.
```

## 7 · Maa ka Haath — "The offering"

**The story.** Every Indian child knows the gesture: a mother's or grandmother's hand reaching out with a **laddu in her palm**, insisting you eat one more. It's love expressed as food, the most honest image of what Immunitywize is.

**The mark.** A **cupped open palm** seen from slightly above, drawn in one continuous line, holding a single **round laddu** (a solid circle). The palm's **heart line** (the crease) continues from the hand into the laddu, connecting giver and gift. The thumb's curve doubles as a subtle **"i"** stroke.

**Construction.**
- Hands are hard, so keep it to **5–6 curves**, with no fingernails and no anatomical detail. Think of a Matisse cut-out rather than an illustration.
- The laddu is the visual anchor: solid, centred, about 35% of the mark's width.
- Test hard at 16 px. If it fails, use only the laddu and the heart-line arc as the small icon.

**Across touchpoints.** The most emotional option, and beautiful as a large stamp on kraft. For the app icon and favicon, use the reduced "laddu + arc" version.

**Motion.** The hand draws in, the laddu drops gently into the palm with a small bounce, and the heart line connects them.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist one-line logo of a gently cupped open palm seen from slightly above, offering a single round Indian sweet (laddu) drawn as a solid saffron #9C4A22 circle resting in the centre of the palm; the palm's heart-line crease continues as a fine curve into the laddu, connecting hand and sweet; only 5 or 6 smooth curves, no fingernails, no anatomical detail, Matisse-like simplicity, uniform espresso stroke on ivory, tender and heartfelt, premium food brand, no text.
```
**Prompt — favicon reduction**
```
Reduced favicon version: a solid saffron circle with a single soft espresso arc cradling it from below, like a palm holding a laddu, flat, bold, legible at 16 pixels, ivory background, no text.
```

## 8 · Jharokha Sunrise — "Before the house wakes up"

**The story.** The website's kitchen animation already tells it: the cooking starts **before the house wakes up**, at the kitchen window, as the sun comes up. The arched window (*jharokha*) is home; the rising sun is a new day's energy and warmth. It's the quiet morning hour when a mother cooks for everyone else.

**The mark.** A tall **arched window** drawn as an outline. Inside, a **half sun** rises from the sill, with 3–5 short rays. The two central mullions are angled very slightly so the framework reads as a hidden **"W"**. Optional: a tiny steam curl crosses one pane.

**Construction.**
- Arch proportion about 1 : 1.5 (width : height), a rounded Mughal-style top kept simple, not ornate.
- The sun is filled in turmeric, everything else in espresso.
- The hidden W should be discoverable, not obvious.

**Across touchpoints.** The arch is iconic and vertical, and doubles as a **window** on packaging: a die-cut arch on the box showing the jar inside. It's also a natural frame for social media photography.

**Motion.** The sun rises inside the window, the rays draw on, and the steam curl drifts across.

**Prompt — symbol**
```
Minimalist logo: a tall arched Indian jharokha window drawn as a clean uniform espresso #2E2119 outline with a simple rounded arch top; inside, a half sun filled in turmeric #D9A441 rising from the window sill with four short rays; the two central window mullions are angled very slightly so the window frame subtly forms a hidden letter W; optionally one tiny curl of steam crossing a pane; flat vector on ivory #FBF8F3, calm morning feeling, warm, premium, no ornament, no text.
```
**Prompt — die-cut box**
```
Premium kraft paper gift box with a die-cut arched window in the shape of a jharokha revealing a glass jar of golden panjiri inside; a small sun motif printed in turmeric above the window; soft morning light, three-quarter product photography, artisanal Indian brand, no readable text.
```

## How to choose

Score each shortlisted mark from 1 to 5:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Meaning | When people hear the story, do they say "oh, that's lovely"? |
| Distinctiveness | Could it be mistaken for any other Indian food brand? (It shouldn't.) |
| Scalability | Does it pass the 16 px, one-colour, seal and app-icon tests? |
| Packaging fit | Does it look at home on a jar lid, a kraft band, a gift box and a wax seal? |
| Motion | Can it animate meaningfully, ideally by being drawn? |
| Longevity | Will it still feel right in 10 years, as the range grows beyond panjiri? |

**Explore first:** **1 Angootha** (most emotional and ownable, and the seal is a ready-made packaging ritual), **2 The Slow Stir** (most connected to the product and the website's line-drawing animation), and **3 Kalava** (expresses care and protection without any medical cliché).

## Next steps

1. Generate 20–40 variations each for the three shortlisted concepts, using the prompts above plus the universal negative prompt.
2. Print the best six at three sizes: 16 px on screen, 4 cm on paper, and full page. Put them on mock jars and phone home screens.
3. Show them to 5–10 real customers without explaining them. Then tell them the story, and note which one they remember the next day.
4. Have a designer redraw the winner as vector, with stroke rules, a small-size variant, clear space, a one-colour version and a lockup with the wordmark.
5. Swap the final logo into `public/favicon.svg`, the header wordmark (`src/components/SiteHeader.tsx`) and the jar label in `src/components/art/ProductSketch.tsx`.
